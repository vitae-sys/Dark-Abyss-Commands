Server Rules

By playing on Dark Abyss you agree to follow these rules. Play fair, respect others, and enjoy your journey through the Abyss.

Core Principle

Play fair. Respect others. Explore the Abyss. Have fun. 🖤

⚖

Respect

How to treat others
  • Treat other players and staff with respect.
  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or targeted abuse.
  • No hate speech, racism, or discrimination.
  • Do not intentionally ruin another player’s experience.
💬

Chat

Keep it clean and readable
  • No spam, flood, or repeated messages.
  • Do not excessively use caps.
  • No advertising other Minecraft servers.
  • No IP advertising or unsolicited server links.
  • Do not intentionally bypass chat filters.
⛏

Griefing

Respect builds and property
  • Do not grief or destroy another player’s builds.
  • Do not steal from protected or claimed areas.
  • Do not intentionally abuse the claim system.
  • Respect other players’ builds and property.
◆

Economy

Keep the economy fair
  • Do not exploit the server economy.
  • Do not duplicate money or items.
  • Do not abuse shops, jobs, rewards, or other economy systems.
  • Report economy exploits to staff.
🛡

Gameplay

Fair play only
  • Do not use cheats, hacked clients, or unfair modifications.
  • No X-ray, killaura, fly, speed, or similar cheats.
  • No autoclickers or macros that provide an unfair advantage.
  • Do not abuse bugs or glitches for personal gain.
  • Do not intentionally cause server lag or crashes.
♛

Staff

Follow staff directions
  • Follow reasonable instructions given by staff.
  • Do not impersonate staff members.
  • Do not abuse the report system.
  • Staff may take action against behavior that harms the server even if it is not explicitly listed above.
⚒

Punishments

Consequences scale by severity
  • Punishments depend on the severity of the offense.
  • Repeated offenses may result in increased punishment.
  • Punishments may include warnings, mutes, kicks, or temporary or permanent bans.
  • Attempting to evade a punishment may result in additional punishment.
▤

Report

Help us protect the server
  • Report bugs, exploits, and cheaters to staff.
  • Provide evidence whenever possible.
  • Do not publicly accuse players without evidence.

Dark Abyss

Play fair. Respect others. Explore the Abyss. Have fun. 🖤
